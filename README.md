# vibecodingfaces

**The faces behind vibe-coded software.** [vibecodingfaces.com](https://vibecodingfaces.com) is a wall of apps people built with AI, each with the face of the person who made it and a link straight to its code.

## Share your app

[**Open the share form →**](https://github.com/rodion-b/vibecodingfaces/issues/new?template=share-app.yml)

It's a short form here on GitHub: the app's name, one line about it, a git link and/or a live link, and what you built it with. Your app shows up on [vibecodingfaces.com](https://vibecodingfaces.com) soon after, with its own page and your GitHub picture.

- **Likes** are ❤️ and 👍 on your app's post. The comments there are its community.
- **To change your app**, edit your post. **To take it down**, close it.
- If the code is on GitHub, your app's page also shows its README, stars and language.

## Ground rules

- Share things you made, with as much help from AI as you like.
- Links must be safe to open: no malware, scams or anything nasty.
- Be kind in the comments.

Apps that break the rules are taken down.
